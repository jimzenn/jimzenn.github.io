{{% definition name="" status="" %}}

Any point satisfying the constraints is called a **feasible point**. The set of all feasible points,

$$\set{ \b{x} \in \R^n : \b{h}(\b{x}) = 0 , \b{g}(\b{x}) \leq \b{0}} $$

is called a **feasible set**.
