{{% definition name="Lagrangian function" status="" %}}

Assume that $f : \R^n \to \R $ and $\b{h} : \R^n \to \R^m $, $f, \b{h} \in \mathcal{ C }^2 $. The Lagrangian function is defined as follow:

$$l(\b{x}, \b{\lambda}) =  f(\b{x}) + \transpose{ \b{\lambda}} \b{h}(\b{x})$$

Let $\b{L}(\b{x}, \b{\lambda}) $ be the Hessian matrix of $l(\b{x}, \b{\lambda}) $ with respect to $\b{x} $: 

$$\b{L}(\b{x}, \b{\lambda}) = \b{F}(\b{x}) + \sum\_{ i }\b{\lambda}\_i\b{H}\_i(\b{x})$$

where $\b{F}(\b{x}) $ is the Hessian matrix of $f $ at $\b{x} $ and $\b{H}\_k(\b{x}) $ is the Hessian matrix of $h\_k $ at $\b{x} $, $k = 1, 2, ..., m $.

{{% /definition %}}



{{% theorem name="Second-Order Necessary Conditions" index="" status="" %}}

Let $\b{x}^\*$ be a local minimizer of $f : \R^n \to \R $ subject to $\b{h}(\b{x}) = \b{0}$, $\b{h}: \R^n \to \R^m $, $m \leq n $, and $f, \b{h} \in \mathcal{ C }^2 $. Suppose that $\b{x}^\* $ is regular. Then, there exists $\lambda^\* \in \R^m$ such that:

1. $Df(\b{x}^\*) + \transpose{ \b{\lambda}^\* } D \b{h}(\b{x}^\*) = \transpose{ \b{0}}$.

2. $\forall \b{y} \in T(\b{x}^\*) , \transpose{ \b{y}} \b{L}(\b{x}^\*, \b{ \lambda }^\*) \b{y} \geq 0$.

{{% /theorem %}}


{{% theorem name="Second-Order Sufficient Conditions" index="" status="" %}}

Let $\b{x}^\*$ be a local minimizer of $f : \R^n \to \R $ subject to $\b{h}(\b{x}) = \b{0}$, $\b{h}: \R^n \to \R^m $, $m \leq n $, and $f, \b{h} \in \mathcal{ C }^2 $. Suppose that $\b{x}^\* $ is regular. Then, there exists $\lambda^\* \in \R^m$ such that:

1. $Df(\b{x}^\*) + \transpose{ \b{\lambda}^\* } D \b{h}(\b{x}^\*) = \transpose{ \b{0}}$.

2. $\forall \b{y} \in T(\b{x}^\*) , \transpose{ \b{y}} \b{L}(\b{x}^\*, \b{ \lambda }^\*) \b{y} > 0$.

Then, $\b{x}^\* $ is a strict local minimizer of $f $ subject to $\b{h}(\b{x}) = \b{0} $.

{{% /theorem %}}
