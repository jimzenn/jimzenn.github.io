**Secant Method** is another search method that can narrow down the minimizer $x^\*$ of a <u>unimodal</u> function $f: \R \to \R$ over a <u>closed</u> interval $[a\_0, b\_0]$.

It is similar to Newton's method, but only needs $f \in \mathcal{C}^1$.

$$x^{(k+1)} = x^{(k)} - \frac{ x^{(k)} - x^{(k-1)}}{ f'(x^{(k)}) - f'(x^{(k-1)})} f'(x^{(k)}) $$

Note that the algorithm requires two initial points to start it, which we denote $x^{(-1)}$ and $x^{(0)}$.
