{{% definition name="Quadratic Programming Problem" status="" %}}

**Quadratic programming problems** has a general form as follow:

$$\begin{align\*}
&\text{minimize }& \frac{ 1 }{ 2 } \transpose{ \b{x}} \b{Q} \b{x} \br
&\text{subject to }& \b{Ax} = \b{b}\br
\end{align\*}$$

where $\b{Q} > 0$, $\b{A} \in \R^{m \times n}$, $m < n$, $\rank{ \b{A}} = m $.

Its global minimizer is 

$$\b{x}^\* = \inv{ \b{Q}} \transpose{ \b{A}} \inv{(\b{A} \inv{ \b{Q}} \transpose{ \b{A}})} \b{b}$$

In the special case where $\b{Q} = \b{I}\_n $, the problem is reduced to the problem described in [section 12.3](optimization/12.3/). 

$$\b{x}^\* = \transpose{ \b{A}} \inv{(\b{A} \transpose{ \b{A}})} \b{b} $$

{{% /definition %}}
