{{% definition name="The Conjugate Gradient Algorithm for QP" status="" %}}

**The conjugate gradient algorithm** does not use prespecified conjugate directions, but instead computes the directions as the algorithm progresses.

We want to minimize the quadratic function of $n$ of variables:

$$ f(x) = \frac{ 1 }{ 2 } \transpose{ \b{x}} \b{Q} \b{x} - \transpose{ \b{x}} \b{b}, \b{x} \in \R^n $$

where $\b{Q} = \transpose{ \b{Q}} > 0.$

{{% /definition %}}
