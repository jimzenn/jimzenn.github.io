$A$ is an event. (underlying phenomenon)
$y$ is a continuous random variable s.t. $f\_{Y \mid A}$ and $f\_{Y|A^c}$ are known (observation)
seek $\b{P}(A| Y = y)$.

{{% example name="" %}}

Binary signal $S $ transmitted, <br>
$\b{P}({S = 1}) = p, \b{P}({S = -1}) = 1 - p$. <br>
received signal is $S+N$, where $N $ is standard normal, $S, N $ are independent.

We seek $\b{P}(S = 1 | Y = y)$ where $Y = S + N $.

$A = \set{ S = 1 }$

$\b{P}(S = 1 | Y = y) = \frac{ \b{P}(S = 1) f\_{Y | S = 1}(y)}{ \b{P}(S = 1)f\_{Y \mid S =1}(y) + \b{P}(S = -1) f\_{ Y | S = -1 }(y)}$

Conditioned on $S = 1, y$ is $N + 1 $,

$f\_{Y | S =1}(y) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}{(1)}}e^{-\frac{(x-{1})^2}{2{(1)}^2}} $

$f\_{Y | S =-1}(y) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}{(1)}}e^{-\frac{(x-{(-1)})^2}{2{(1)}^2}}$

{{% /example %}}

{{% theorem name="" index=" " %}}

$$\b{P}(A | Y = y) = \frac{ \b{P}(A)f\_{Y \mid A}(y)}{ \b{P}(A)f\_{Y \mid A}(y) + \b{P}(A^c)f\_{Y \mid A^c}(y)} = \frac{ \b{P}(A)f\_{Y \mid A}(y)}{ f\_Y{y}}$$

{{% /theorem %}}
